# Faceless 2 — The Signal Below / The Return

Original atmospheric Backrooms investigation game by **Zorix GAme Team**. The supplied game icon, team logo, literal `Made By Zorix GAme Team` intro and [zorix.it](https://zorix.it) are preserved. Godot **4.7.2**.

## v0.15: explore the story, not a tape-repair minigame

You are a Zorix field investigator tracing Lin Lan's signal to an office block demolished five years ago. Three story chapters reveal an impossible demolition order, water that runs backwards, a third crew member wearing your uniform, a flooded identity card, and a time-loop radio warning. Each chapter has **three distinct physical investigation props** and an evidence journal (`J` / touch JOURNAL), with chapter-break commentary. The last lift presents **two choices**: send the evidence or seal the signal. The host validates and synchronizes evidence and the ending for up to four ENet/UDP peers. Sightings remain brief, locally directed, non-combat and always manually interruptible.

### Authored GLB visual geometry

Visible world geometry comes from *licensed authored* GLB meshes: Huuxloc's BackRooms architecture (CC BY 4.0), Poly Haven furniture (CC0), and the skinned HorrorGameMaker monster (CC0). The first-person forearms **and visible lower legs/feet** are skin-weighted original triangle subsets of Cesium Man (CC BY 4.0), with its real rig and walk animation intact. The extraction is deterministic in `faceless2/create_viewmodel.py`. No visible primitive meshes are generated by GDScript. Invisible boxes/capsules are retained only for efficient physics collisions.

**Every level includes flooded ground**, not just the pool. Authored GLB water surfaces share a budgeted animated shader with irregular alpha shorelines, Fresnel/specular response and capped footstep ripples (eight active impulses, seven reused splash droplets derived from the source GLB). This is a mobile-friendly approximation, not realtime path-traced reflections.

### Audio, networking, mobile

Nineteen Mandarin story voice clips are synthesized **offline in CI** using the Apache-2.0 Kokoro Chinese neural voice model and included as OGG files in the APK. No Android/system TTS and no fal.ai. Footsteps, breath, mechanics and water are spatial or recorded/generated in advance. Responsive touch HUD, safe-area scaling, three dynamic-resolution presets and a bounded local shadow budget remain in place.

Co-op: direct UDP/ENet on port 24711 by default, 1–4 players, protocol 15. No hosted relay, universal NAT traversal or host migration. Story shares the host's decisions; monster sightings and camera rotation do not drive remote peers.

### Verification and release scope

`.github/workflows/faceless2-apk.yml` performs SHA-verified external asset restoration, generates audio/limb derivatives, imports through Godot headless, runs level/story/cinematic and **separate real ENet process** network checks, and only then exports `faceless2-v015-zorix.apk` (Android 0.15.0, code 15). No APK installation or runtime testing, graphics screenshots, Android emulators, Xvfb or Vulkan image testing.

Headless verification cannot establish actual visual polish, phone frame rate, thermals, touch behavior, accessibility or commercial AAA quality. These remain device/visual QA tasks. This remains a horror game prototype and has not been merged into `main`.
